Why some cities cool down faster

Field notes · 6 min read

Urban planners have long known that trees mitigate summer heat, but new measurements show the effect is far from uniform. Neighbourhoods with older canopy recover faster after a heatwave, and their residents report fewer sleepless nights.

The researchers argue that a resilient street is not simply a green one. Shade, soil moisture and the width of the road all interact, and a single missing piece can curtail the benefit of everything else.

Their proposal is pragmatic: measure each block, plant where the gain is largest, and revisit the data every summer instead of relying on a single citywide average.
